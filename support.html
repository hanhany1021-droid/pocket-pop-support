Support

Optional material purchases are being prepared for version 1.5. They may not yet be available in your installed version.

Popwrap is a tactile toy for iPhone and iPad. The classic material is free and play works offline. Optional wood and metal materials can be tried before a one-time purchase.

No haptic feedback?

Use a physical iPhone that supports Core Haptics. The iOS Simulator cannot reproduce the tactile experience. Also check that System Haptics is enabled in Settings and that Low Power Mode or other system conditions are not limiting feedback.

No sound?

Open the top-right Settings button and turn Sound on, then check your device volume and audio output.

Try a material before buying

Open the material button at the top of the play screen. Choose wood or metal and try the two bubbles in Press, Sweep or Infinite mode as often as you like. Preview pops do not count toward your statistics or achievements. A purchase unlocks full sheets in every size and mode for the included material.

Purchases and restores

Purchases and restores require an internet connection and are handled by Apple. Prices are shown in your App Store currency. If you have already bought a material, tap Restore Purchases on the material page using the same Apple Account. Popwrap does not receive your payment credentials or send your Apple ID to the developer.

The bundle is available when neither material is owned. After buying one material separately, you can buy the other only at its individual price; there is no top-up offer. If a purchase is pending approval or cannot be completed, you can continue using the classic material.

Report a problem

Open an issue in the Popwrap support tracker. Include your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, app version, and the steps that reproduce the problem. The tracker is hosted by GitHub and may be public. Do not include your Apple ID, receipts, payment details, or other personal or sensitive information.
