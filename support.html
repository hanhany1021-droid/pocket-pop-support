POCKET POP

Support

Pocket Pop is a fully offline tactile toy for iPhone.

No haptic feedback?

Use a physical iPhone that supports Core Haptics. The iOS Simulator cannot reproduce the tactile experience. Also check that System Haptics is enabled in Settings and that Low Power Mode or other system conditions are not limiting feedback.

No sound?

Tap the speaker button in the top-right corner, then check your device volume and Silent mode.

Report a problem

Open an issue in the Pocket Pop support tracker. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, app version, and the steps that reproduce the problem. Do not include personal or sensitive information.
