Privacy Policy

Updated October 29, 2026

Optional material purchases are being prepared for version 1.5. They may not yet be available in your installed version.

Summary

The Popwrap developer does not collect, upload, sell, or share personal data or gameplay activity through the app. Gameplay runs on your device. Optional purchases and restores are processed by Apple.

How the app works

The app's bubble wrap interactions, sound, and haptic feedback run locally on your device and can be used offline. Settings, onboarding preferences, pop counts and achievements are stored on your device. Popwrap does not require an app account and does not include analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party analytics SDKs.

Apple purchases

Loading prices, purchasing optional materials and restoring purchases use Apple's StoreKit service and require an internet connection. Apple handles the payment and account information under its own privacy policy. The app checks Apple's verified transactions on the device to determine which materials you own and to reflect refunds or revoked purchases.

The developer does not receive your Apple Account password or payment card details. Your Apple ID and transaction history are not uploaded to a developer-operated server. Popwrap does not operate an account, payment or analytics server.

Device permissions

Popwrap does not request access to your camera, microphone, photos, location, contacts, health information, or other personal device data.

Children's privacy

The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children through Popwrap. Optional purchases are handled by Apple, including any purchase-approval controls configured for the Apple Account.

Changes to this policy

If a future version changes how data is handled, this page and the App Store privacy disclosure will be updated before that version is released.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be submitted through the Popwrap support tracker. This external service is operated by GitHub and issues may be public. Do not post Apple IDs, receipts, payment information or other sensitive data.
