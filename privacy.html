POCKET POP

Privacy Policy

Effective August 27, 2026

Summary

Pocket Pop does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share personal data or usage data.

How the app works

The app's bubble wrap interactions, sound, and haptic feedback run locally on your device. Pocket Pop does not require an account and does not include analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party software development kits.

Device permissions

Pocket Pop does not request access to your camera, microphone, photos, location, contacts, health information, or other personal device data.

Children's privacy

Because Pocket Pop does not collect data from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version changes how data is handled, this page and the App Store privacy disclosure will be updated before that version is released.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be submitted through the Pocket Pop support tracker.
