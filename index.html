POPWRAP

A tactile bubble wrap toy for iPhone and iPad.

Optional material purchases are being prepared for version 1.5. They may not yet be available in your installed version.

Press, sweep, and pop transparent bubbles with synchronized sound and haptics. The classic material is free and play works offline. Wood and metal are optional one-time purchases, with two bubbles to try before buying.

Purchases and restores need an internet connection and are handled by Apple.
